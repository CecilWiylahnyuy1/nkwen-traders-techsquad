Our story

Thirty years of stocking what people actually need

Nkwen Traders opened its doors in 1994 as a single shopfront in the Nkwen neighborhood of Bamenda, founded by Mama Beatrice Ngwa with a simple idea: a shop should carry what a household runs out of every week, priced fairly, and restocked without fail.

"If a mother sends her child for semovita and palm oil, that child should not come home empty-handed. That is the whole business." — Mama Beatrice Ngwa, Founder

What started as one shop has grown into six branches across the Northwest region, but the standard has not changed. Every branch carries the same core pantry staples, and every branch manager is trained to know their regular customers by name.

How we grew

  • 1994 Nkwen Main branch opens in Bamenda with a single storeroom of pantry staples.
  • 2003 The Kondengui branch opens, and the weekly chin-chin bake becomes a neighborhood fixture.
  • 2011 Nkwen Traders partners directly with honey cooperatives near Bafut to open its specialty aisle.
  • 2019 A sixth branch opens, and Nkwen Traders begins sourcing Bamenda highland coffee in small batches.
  • 2026 Nkwen Traders launches its first public website — the one you're looking at now.

What we stand for

Trustworthy pricing, dependable stock, and a specialty in the everyday goods and locally loved snacks that keep a household running. That's the whole philosophy, thirty years running.
